Educational first-order model. No claimed biological energy production, extraordinary efficiency, radiation tolerance, material-density modulation or validated real-world reactor.

05 / Research boundary

Established engineering
Photovoltaic generation, finite battery storage, temperature derating, converter losses, closed-loop sensing and control.
Testable software analogy
State A (steady), State B (high load), State C (protective/adaptive) represented by power-converter modes and alternative control strategies.
Unverified materials hypothesis
Tardigrade-inspired molecular protection may inspire protective coatings, but no biological layer is assumed to generate energy or impart radiation tolerance.
What this simulator cannot establish
No physical component validation, no hardware-safe setpoints, no learned generalization guarantees, and no proof of any performance numbers in the original blueprint.
