The Third Relationship

ΦREL-369: three domains, six directed interactions, nine matrix channels, and an optional higher-order rule. Explore the difference between what pairs can tell us and what a joint interaction reveals.

Research status: Mathematical demonstration and toy dynamical model, not new physics, quantum advantage, consciousness, or a proven superiority of 3-6-9.

Experiment A. Information hidden in a three-way constraint

Let A and B be independent fair bits. Let C follow A XOR B with configurable strength. Even when XOR always holds, all three pairs are statistically independent. The full triple is not.

Interpretation and controls

The first experiment uses an exact 8-state probability distribution, not sampling. The second uses a simple dimensionless update law, not an electric circuit or physical energy model. The higher-order rule injects or removes modeled state, so only the pairwise-only model is expected to conserve the state sum.

For the source code, test cases, assumptions, and instructions on comparing this with other architectures, read the research notes. The whole implementation is dependency-free and inspectable.
